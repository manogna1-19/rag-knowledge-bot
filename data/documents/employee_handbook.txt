TECHNOVA SOLUTIONS — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

Company Overview

TechNova Solutions is a technology company that develops software products and provides technology services to businesses. The company promotes collaboration, innovation, continuous learning, and professional growth.

Working Hours

Standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Employees are expected to maintain their assigned working schedule. Flexible working arrangements may be available depending on the employee's role and manager approval.

Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 24 paid leave days per calendar year. Leave should normally be requested at least three working days in advance through the company's HR portal.

For emergency leave, employees should inform their reporting manager as soon as possible.

Unused leave may be carried forward according to the company's annual leave policy.

Work From Home Policy

Employees may request work-from-home arrangements when their role permits remote work. Work-from-home requests require approval from the employee's reporting manager.

Employees working remotely are expected to remain available during normal working hours and attend scheduled meetings.

Attendance

Employees are expected to maintain regular attendance and communicate with their manager if they are unable to report to work.

Repeated unapproved absences may be reviewed by the employee's manager and HR department.

Employee Benefits

Eligible full-time employees receive benefits including health insurance, paid leave, and access to company learning and development programs.

Benefits may vary depending on employment type and applicable company policies.

Learning and Development

TechNova Solutions encourages employees to improve their technical and professional skills. Employees may participate in internal training programs, workshops, technical sessions, and approved external learning programs.

Employees should discuss relevant training opportunities with their managers.

Code of Conduct

Employees are expected to maintain professional behavior, respect colleagues, protect company information, and follow applicable company policies.

Confidential company information must not be shared with unauthorized individuals.

Performance Reviews

Employee performance is reviewed periodically by managers. Performance reviews may consider work quality, productivity, teamwork, communication, technical skills, and achievement of assigned goals.

Employees are encouraged to discuss career development goals with their managers.
